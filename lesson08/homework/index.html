about bmw

The BMW M4 Competition is a high-performance coupe known for its powerful engine, dynamic handling, and aggressive styling. It's a step up from the standard M4, offering more horsepower and performance-oriented features. The M4 Competition typically features a 3.0-liter inline-6 engine with BMW's M TwinPower Turbo technology, producing 510 hp. It comes standard with an 8-speed M Steptronic transmission and can be optioned with M xDrive all-wheel drive for enhanced traction and handling.
